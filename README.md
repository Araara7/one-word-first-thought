Exactly one word — the first word that comes to mind — for the [One Word, First Thought](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8) bounty on make.fun.

## The word

> defenestration

Fourteen letters. The act of throwing someone out of a window: surprising, vivid, and funny in a single word — the image lands the instant you read it, and the sound of the word itself is half the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically (two runs produce byte-identical output).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
